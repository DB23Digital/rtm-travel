## 1. The Revenue Opportunity (Leak Math)

Anthea, your website is technically well-built, loads quickly, and contains high-quality corporate travel guides that show your 18 years of industry experience. However, there are **four hidden conversion leaks** on the site that are quietly costing you new corporate bookings. 

In B2B corporate travel, a single SME account is highly valuable:
*   An average South African SME travels regularly, spending between **R500,000 and R2,000,000** annually on flights, hotels, and car rentals.
*   Through transaction fees and volume rebates, a single account generates between **R30,000 and R120,000** in high-margin service revenue for your agency.
*   **The Leaks:** 
    1.  **No WhatsApp Connection:** EAs and procurement leads in South Africa run on WhatsApp. The site says "Send a quick WhatsApp", but there is no link or button to click. Mobile buyers leave when forced to fill in long forms.
    2.  **Google Maps Invisibility:** Your website does not declare coordinate locations, and you have no visible Google Maps listing. EAs searching for a "corporate travel agent Cape Town" cannot find you.
    3.  **No Stated Response Guarantee:** CFOs and EAs switch travel agents because of slow service. Your contact page does not state how fast you respond, missing the chance to advertise your under-5-minute follow-up.
    4.  **Zero Proof:** There are no client reviews or testimonials on the site. B2B buyers and AI search engines (like ChatGPT) will not recommend a service with no visible proof.

### The Leak Math:
If these four leaks cause just **2 corporate travel managers** per month to exit your site and book with a competitor:
*   You lose **R100,000 per month** in potential agency revenue.
*   Over a year, this compounds to **R1.2 Million in lost B2B contracts**.

*By implementing the DB23 Lead Engine and fixing these leaks, we can plug these revenue gaps and capture Cape Town's high-value corporate travel market.*

## 4. What We Are Deliberately NOT Doing (June 2026 Rules)

We adhere strictly to modern search standards and will not waste your budget on ineffective tactics:
1.  **NO FAQPage Schema:** Google officially removed FAQ rich results for all commercial sites. We will not write or update FAQPage schema.
2.  **NO llms.txt Promotion:** While a `llms.txt` file exists on the server, Google Search Central docs officially confirm it has no effect on Google AI Overviews. We will not allocate resources to expand it.
3.  **NO Fake Reviews:** We will not fabricate reviews or testimonials, as this violates CPA/POPIA regulations in South Africa and FTC rules. All trust signals will be gathered from your real corporate accounts.
