Privacy Policy

How RTM Travel (Remmitz Travel Management) collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Draft notice: This policy has been prepared from RTM Travel's actual data practices on this website (the contact form, Google Analytics, and WhatsApp enquiries) to close a live compliance gap. It has not yet been reviewed by the client or a legal professional. Treat it as a working draft, and update or replace this page once that review is complete.

RTM Travel (Remmitz Travel Management), a South African business travel management company, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA). This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

1. Who We Are

RTM Travel (Remmitz Travel Management) is a corporate travel management company based in Cape Town, South Africa, operating in association with eTravel and accredited by ASATA and IATA. For the purposes of POPIA, RTM Travel is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect personal information in the following ways:

  • →Contact form: first name, last name, contact number, company name, work email address, enquiry subject, and any message you choose to include.
  • →WhatsApp enquiries: your phone number and the content of messages you send us via WhatsApp.
  • →Website analytics: we use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect standard technical data such as pages visited, time on site, device type, and approximate location, in order to understand how the website is used.

We do not knowingly collect payment card details, ID numbers, or passport details through this website. Where travel bookings require that information, it is collected through separate, direct channels with our travel consultants — not through this website.

3. Why We Collect It

We process your personal information to:

  • →Respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form or WhatsApp;
  • →Prepare quotations and provide corporate travel management services you have requested;
  • →Understand and improve how visitors use this website; and
  • →Meet our legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

Our lawful basis for this processing is your consent (given when you submit the contact form or message us), and, where you become a client, the performance of a contract for travel management services.

4. Who We Share It With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary to operate our business:

  • →Google (Analytics, and WhatsApp/Meta for messaging): as the operators of the platforms used to run this site and receive your enquiries. These providers may process data outside South Africa under their own privacy terms.
  • →Airlines, hotels, and other travel suppliers: only once you become a client and only to the extent required to book and manage your travel.
  • →Regulators or authorities: where required by law.

5. How Long We Keep It

We retain contact form and WhatsApp enquiry information for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, for the duration of our business relationship plus any period required by tax, accounting, or other legal obligations. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • →Request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • →Request that we correct or update inaccurate information;
  • →Request that we delete information we no longer have a lawful basis to hold;
  • →Object to our processing of your information, or withdraw consent at any time; and
  • →Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe we have not handled your information lawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 8 below.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information we hold from loss, unauthorised access, or disclosure. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
